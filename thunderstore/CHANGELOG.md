## 1.3.1
- Hold hands and twirl: a little more room between you than in 1.3.0 (which was too close), still with straight arms
  pointing down and the hands meeting in the middle.

## 1.3.0
- Hold hands and twirl: you now stand close enough that your arms stay straight and point down, and the near hands just
  swing sideways to meet. Your heads tip away from each other more, as the big heads are close.

## 1.2.0
- Twirl keeps going until one of you walks away (no time limit), like hold hands.
- Side hug: you stand closer together, each with a hand on the other's waist, and your heads tip away from each other
  so the big heads don't sink into each other.

## 1.1.0
- Removed the fist bump (too close to the high five).
- Hold hands: you stand closer together with your elbows bent, your heads tip away from each other, and it
  lasts until one of you walks away (no time limit). The twirl uses the same closer stance.
- Needs Hugs and More 1.3.1 or newer.

## 1.0.0
- First version: an addon for Hugs and More that adds five more two-player emotes to its F menu:
  **side hug**, **hold hands**, **fist bump**, **twirl** and **bow**.
- Needs Hugs and More 1.3.0 or newer, and both players need Alternate Poses for these emotes to show up.
