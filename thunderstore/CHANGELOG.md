## 1.3.1
Alternate Poses is an addon for [Hugs and More](https://thunderstore.io/c/webfishing/p/Fayt/Hugs_and_More/) and needs it
(version 1.3.1 or newer). It adds these emotes to Hugs and More's **F** menu:
- **Side hug**: side by side, each puts a hand on the other's waist, with a gentle sway and hearts.
- **Hold hands**: side by side with straight arms pointing down, the near hands meet and swing a little. It lasts until one of you walks away.
- **Twirl**: holding hands, you keep turning around each other until one of you walks away.
- **Bow**: face to face, you both bow.
